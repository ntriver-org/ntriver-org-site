## Info

#### What is Windows 10 China Government Edition (CMGE)?  
Windows 10 China Government Edition (CMGE) was developed by [CMIT](https://www.cmgos.com/web/product_en/overview_en/), a joint venture between [Microsoft](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/2017/05/23/announcing-windows-10-china-government-edition-new-surface-pro/) and [CETC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Electronics_Technology_Group_Corporation).  
It was designed specifically for the Chinese government and state-owned entities, and complies fully with local laws, regulations, and security standards. It is a heavily modified operating system based on the `EnterpriseG` edition ID.

**CETC** = China Electronics Technology Group Corporation  
**CMIT** = CETC & Microsoft Information Technologies  
**CMGE** = CM (CMIT) GE (Government Edition)

**Windows 10 China Government Edition (CMGE) - Official website:**  
https://www.cmgos.com/web/product_en/overview_en/

#### How to download the Windows 10 China Government Edition (CMGE) ISO from the official site?

* Go to the website: https://download.cmgos.com/oem/login
* Use the following serials and enter the captcha to get the download links:
  * `0000010000000000` (16 digits)
  * `0101000000000` (13 digits)
  * `0502200011367` (13 digits)
